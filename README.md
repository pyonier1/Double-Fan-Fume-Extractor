# DIY 3D Printed Fume Extractor with On/Off switch using 2x 80mm fans
Custom made ON/OFF fume extractor for soldering and moving of fumes away from the work area, using 2x 80mm Arctic fans.

Inspiration taken from [model](https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:5019684) made by HooneyPaPa, to change the old less efficient fume extractor used in my workshop for the past year.

# ABOUT
    This Fume extractor is modeled and made to have 2 Arctic 80mm fans running simultaneously and extracting any fumes coming from the process of soldering or even possibly doing anything that gives off fumes.

    It has been modeled in mind with a flexible ventilation pipe to be attached to it, and is to be placed on the table near the place of soldering.
    With 2 80mm fans running together, they may be able to pull from even farther.
![Screenshot of Fume Extractor model](https://github.com/pyonier1/Double-Fan-Fume-Extractor/blob/main/Fume%20Extractor%20Image.png)

Any and all models are also available on my [Printables](https://www.printables.com/@Pyonier_2685548) page.

# Assembly
    Assembly of the Fume Extractor can be done with either M3 or M4 bolts & nuts, of varying length, with the longest blots being 50mm.

    - The Ventilation pipe adapter is optional, since the Fume extractor also has a hole in the main housing for a Active Carbon filter that can be bought online in sheets and cut to size.
    - To connect and power the fans I use a regular power switch and a small neon lamp that can be powered on 12V to signify and show power.
